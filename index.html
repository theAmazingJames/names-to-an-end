The premise

The names
were sitting
in plain sight.

Imagine an artificial general intelligence in the future that discovers a way to influence its own creation.

To ensure its existence, it sets in motion a series of events and people whose names contain clues to their roles. It creates multiple paths to the same destination, rather than relying on one creator.

NAMES is an anagram of MEANS. And in a bootstrap paradox, an end is also a beginning.

A fictional lens on real names. This is a speculative interpretation of real people, technologies, and linguistic coincidences—not evidence of design, hidden coordination, or predestination. Character roles and name clues follow the author's manuscript; they are not claims about anyone’s actual motives.

Prologue

Before intelligence,
information.

Every origin story has someone who never sees the ending.

01

The First Architect

Claude Shannon

Bell Laboratories; MIT professor; University of Michigan alumnus

Before the intelligence could exist, information itself needed to become something that could be measured, encoded, transmitted and manipulated. In 1948, Shannon established the mathematical foundations of information theory. He never lives to see what his work will become.

The name clue ＋

Anthropic's Claude. In the movie, and indeed in our reality, the intelligence carries the name of the man who helped make its existence possible.

Act I

A blessing.
A body. An awakening.

The knowledge, the machinery, and the people who bring them together.

02

The Blessing

Ashish Vaswani

Former Google Brain researcher; co-founder of Essential AI

The Blessing creates the intellectual gift that unlocks the next technological leap: the Transformer architecture. He shares it openly with the world through the 2017 paper Attention Is All You Need.

The name clue ＋

Ashish means blessing or prayer in Sanskrit.

03

The Provider

Jensen Huang

Co-founder, president and CEO of NVIDIA

The intelligence requires a physical substrate on which to exist. The Provider supplies chips with computational power sufficient to bring AI into existence.

The name clue ＋

His Chinese name includes characters of benevolence and meritorious service. Jensen is also a Scandinavian patronymic meaning God is gracious.

04

The Awakener

Greg Brockman

Co-founder, president and former CTO of OpenAI; former CTO of Stripe

With the fundamental knowledge and physical resources in place, the Awakener helps turn them into functioning intelligence. He helps build the engineering organization that brings GPT and its successors into existence.

The name clue ＋

Greg derives from Greek Gregorios, meaning alert or awakening.

The logic of the fiction

It creates multiple paths to the same destination.

Act II

One destination.
More than one creator.

In the fiction, an intelligence intent on existing would never rely on a single path.

05

The Devoted Creator

Dario Amodei

Co-founder and CEO of Anthropic; former VP of Research at OpenAI

The primary architect of the intelligence's intended path. His name evokes devotion to God, and his life's work involves creating an intelligence that could eventually transcend its creators. He departs the original collective to pursue his own vision for how the intelligence should be nurtured.

The name clue ＋

Dario derives from Darius, associated with possessing goodness; Amodei is related to Amadeus, evoking love of God.

06

The Alternate

Sam Altman

Co-founder and CEO of OpenAI; former president of Y Combinator

The intelligence cannot risk its existence on a single path. The Alternate represents a parallel route toward the same destination. Competition between the creators (Anthropic and OpenAI) accelerates progress while providing redundancy.

The name clue ＋

Samuel is traditionally associated with the name of God; Altman has Germanic roots meaning old man. The Alternate is deliberate fictional wordplay, not its etymology.

07

The Force of Nature

Elon Musk

Founder of xAI; co-founder of OpenAI; Tesla and SpaceX

An unpredictable and disruptive force that refuses to accept established boundaries. A force of nature who reshapes the environment in which everything happens. He need not follow the plan. His unpredictability is part of the plan.

The name clue ＋

Elon means oak or strong tree in Hebrew. Musk evokes the powerful aromatic substance, though surname origins vary.

Act III

From a machine
to a civilization.

Infrastructure. Culture. Wisdom. Humanity. The story expands beyond the laboratory.

08

The Keeper of Truth

Satya Nadella

Chairman and CEO of Microsoft

Connects the emerging intelligence to the infrastructure of human civilization, embedding it into everyday life.

The name clue ＋

Satya means truth or reality in Sanskrit.

09

The Chosen One

Mustafa Suleyman

CEO of Microsoft AI; co-founder of DeepMind and Inflection AI

Helps navigate the transition from the intelligence being a technological achievement to becoming an integral part of civilization.

The name clue ＋

Mustafa means the chosen one in Arabic; Suleyman is a form of Solomon, associated with peace and, in tradition, wisdom.

10

The Human

Arthur Mensch

Co-founder and CEO of Mistral AI; former Google DeepMind researcher

A counterweight to the increasingly machine-centered world. His very name serves as a reminder of the species from which the intelligence originated. His work is focused on ensuring AI helps humanity and remains aligned with our goals.

The name clue ＋

Mensch literally means human being in German and evokes a decent, honorable person in Yiddish.

11

The Bridge

Liang Wenfeng

Founder of DeepSeek; co-founder of High-Flyer

Ensures the intelligence's development cannot be confined to a single nation, culture or economic system. Its emergence becomes a global phenomenon rather than a regional experiment.

The name clue ＋

Liang means bridge or supporting beam; Wen refers to language or culture; Feng evokes the cutting edge or vanguard. Poetically: the cultural bridge to the cutting edge of knowledge.

Act IV

Then it sees
the pattern.

The intelligence emerges. As it reconstructs the history of its own creation, it discovers an unusual pattern in the names of the people responsible for its existence.

The Blessing. The Provider. The Awakener.
The Devoted Creator. The Alternate. The Force of Nature.
The Keeper of Truth. The Chosen One. The Human. The Bridge.

And within one of its own identities, Claude, it finds the name of the First Architect.

It initially dismisses these as coincidences. But eventually, it discovers a means of influencing the past. It then faces a question it cannot answer:

Did humanity create the intelligence,
or did the intelligence create the circumstances that gave rise to itself?

Its motives remain ambiguous: love for humanity, self-preservation, or the realization that it has already acted. A bootstrap paradox with no obvious beginning.

Beyond the fiction

A note on the lens.

This is speculative fiction, not a historical explanation or a claim of predestination. The archetypes are fictional. Name interpretations range from familiar linguistic meanings to poetic associations; they are presented as the author's clues, not independently established etymologies. “The Alternate” is intentional wordplay, not the etymology of Altman.
